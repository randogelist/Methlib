COLLATZ_AFFINE_INTEGER_V1: Exact witness interchange format

Tokens are separated by whitespace; labels are case sensitive and must occur
exactly in the indicated order. Integers use canonical signed base-10 text:
0, a positive integer without leading zeroes, or - followed by a positive
integer without leading zeroes. No plus signs, decimal points or -0.

COLLATZ_AFFINE_INTEGER_V1
dimension d
denominator D
B0 matrix <d*d integers, row-major> offset <d integers>
B1 matrix <d*d integers, row-major> offset <d integers>
G0 matrix <d*d integers, row-major> offset <d integers>
G1 matrix <d*d integers, row-major> offset <d integers>
G2 matrix <d*d integers, row-major> offset <d integers>
seed <d integers>
weight <d integers>
END

Decoder uses E.den = D, the five integer matrices and offsets exactly as
listed, and seed and weight exactly as listed. Denominator positivity,
nonnegativity of B0 and B1, and ALL other arithmetic constraints are checked
by Lean's IntegerValid predicate, not by the parser. G values may be signed.

Current generator implementation supports 1 <= d <= 256, at most 64 MiB per
input file, and at most 100000 characters per integer token. Invalid format or
resource limits are generation errors, not mathematical rejection. A failing
or interrupted Lean run is not automatically a certified rejection either.

EXAMPLE_INVALID_WITNESS.txt is deliberately mathematically invalid; it parses
but Lean's `decide` must fail because the gap condition is false. It is not a
Collatz certificate. EXAMPLE_MALFORMED_WITNESS.txt must be rejected by the
parser. There is no accepted witness in this package.

Generated source uses `by decide`, not `native_decide`. Lean must kernel-check
the entire predicate and audit the final theorem. Large inputs may exceed
resources or practical limits; this generator does not guarantee a fast check.
