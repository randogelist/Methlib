R7.ROOT.2. B-ONLY REFINEMENT: A GOOD TIME WITH TWO EXPLICIT MARGINS

Fix delta>tau and set
  C := 2*pi*nu^2,
  T := (delta+tau)/2,
  q := (T+tau)/(2*T) = (delta+3*tau)/(2*(delta+tau)).

Claims:
  0 <= tau < T < delta;
  1/2 <= q < 1;
  q*T=(T+tau)/2>tau;
  C>0;
  C^2*tau=||a||_2^4/(2*nu).

Proof of scalar claims.
The denominator of tau is positive. Hence tau>=0. From delta>tau,
T-tau=(delta-tau)/2>0 and delta-T=(delta-tau)/2>0; thus T>0.
The identity q=1/2+tau/(2*T) yields q>=1/2. The inequality tau<T
gives q<1. Multiplication by T yields q*T=(T+tau)/2>tau.
The last identity follows by C^2=4*pi^2*nu^4 and cancellation of the
strictly positive scalar 8*pi^2*nu^5. No amplitude restriction occurs.

Apply LH_FOURTH_POWER_BUDGET on the shortened interval (0,T):
  H_u^2 is integrable on (0,T),
  integral_(0,T) H_u(r)^2 dr <= ||a||_2^4/(2*nu)=C^2*tau
    < C^2*q*T.

Apply exists_good_time_of_integral_lt with
  f(r)=H_u(r)^2,
  c=C^2*q,
  Good as in R7.ROOT.1.
All inputs are explicit: T>0, c>=0, integrability, the a.e. restart
predicate, and the displayed strict integral inequality.
It supplies s with
  0<s<T,
  Good(s),
  H_u(s)^2<C^2*q.

Since Good(s) includes CriticalPhysicalH1 (u(s)) (G(s)),
  physicalHalfEnergy(u(s)) != infinity
by physicalH1_halfEnergy_ne_top. Therefore the real readout does not
erase an infinite extended-energy value at the selected witness.

Let rho:=sqrt(q). Then rho>=0, rho^2=q, and 0<rho<1.
If H_u(s)>=C*rho, squaring the nonnegative right-hand side gives
  H_u(s)^2 >= C^2*rho^2=C^2*q,
contradicting the selected inequality. Consequently
  H_u(s)<C*rho<C.
Also
  delta-s>delta-T=(delta-tau)/2.

Thus the full output is
  exists s in (0,(delta+tau)/2),
    Good(s)
    and H_u(s)<2*pi*nu^2*sqrt(q)<2*pi*nu^2
    and delta-s>(delta-tau)/2.

The extended-valued admission inequality follows from finiteness and
ENNReal.ofReal_toReal / ENNReal.ofReal_lt_ofReal_iff:
  physicalHalfEnergy(u(s)) < ENNReal.ofReal(2*pi*nu^2).
