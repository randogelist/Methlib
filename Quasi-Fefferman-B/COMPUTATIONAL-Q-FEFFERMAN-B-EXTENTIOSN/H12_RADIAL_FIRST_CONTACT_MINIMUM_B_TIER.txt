0. PURPOSE
----------

This file replaces the informal word "angle" by the exact radial optimization
object required by the IMPROVEMENT program.

The object to identify at radius r is:

  the normalized boundary datum on the H^(1/2) sphere that minimizes the
  certified local-window / QFB-overlap ratio.

The root mechanism is:

  minimum overlap ratio > 1

  -> the current admitted ball absorbs its boundary and a nontrivial outer
     shell

  -> the admission radius increases.

The obstruction to arbitrary growth is a finite first-contact radius at which
the minimum overlap ratio reaches 1.


1. VISCOSITY NORMALIZATION
--------------------------

Let

  X := H^(1/2)_{sigma,0}(T^3)

with homogeneous critical norm

  ||b||_X := ||b||_{Ḣ^(1/2)}.

For viscosity nu > 0, write physical data as

  b = nu * r * omega,

where

  r > 0,
  omega in X,
  ||omega||_X = 1.

Define the normalized critical sphere

  Sigma := { omega in X : ||omega||_X = 1 }.

The physical H^(1/2) radius is

  R = nu*r.

The IMPROVEMENT factor corresponding to r is

  I(r) := r^2 / (2*pi).

Indeed,

  R^2 = nu^2*r^2 = 2*pi*I(r)*nu^2.

Therefore

  r -> infinity

is equivalent to

  I -> infinity.

For a viscosity-nu solution u, set

  s := nu*t,
  v(s,x) := u(s/nu,x)/nu.

Then v solves the viscosity-one NSE.  If

  S_*(r,omega)

is the maximal viscosity-one strong lifespan generated by r*omega, then the
physical lifespan satisfies

  T_*^nu(nu*r*omega) = S_*(r,omega)/nu.

This removes nu from the radial optimization problem.


2. QFB OVERLAP AGAINST AN INHERITED RADIUS
------------------------------------------

Assume that the physical ball of radius

  R_0 = nu*r_0

is already admitted:

  ADMISSION_RADIUS_nu(r_0):

  forall b in X,

    ||b||_X < nu*r_0

    ->

    Reg_nu(b).

For a datum

  b = nu*r*omega,

its QFB cutoff inherited from r_0 is

  T_QFB(r_0; r,omega,nu)

  := ||b||_2^4 / (2*nu*(nu*r_0)^4)

   = r^4*||omega||_2^4 / (2*nu*r_0^4).

In normalized time s = nu*t, this becomes

  S_QFB(r_0; r,omega)

  := nu*T_QFB

   = r^4*||omega||_2^4 / (2*r_0^4).

Therefore a strong window of normalized length S certifies the datum whenever

  S > r^4*||omega||_2^4 / (2*r_0^4).


3. TRUE AND CERTIFIED RADIAL PERMEABILITY
-----------------------------------------

Define the true radial permeability

  Q_*(r,omega)

  := 2*S_*(r,omega) / ||omega||_2^4,

with value +infinity when the datum is globally strong.

Then

  Q_*(r,omega) > (r/r_0)^4

is exactly the QFB overlap condition relative to the inherited radius r_0.

For computation, let

  S_lower(r,omega)

be any rigorous lower bound for the normalized strong lifespan:

  0 < S_lower(r,omega) <= S_*(r,omega).

Define the certified radial permeability

  Q_lower(r,omega)

  := 2*S_lower(r,omega) / ||omega||_2^4.

Then

  Q_lower(r,omega) > (r/r_0)^4

implies

  Q_*(r,omega) > (r/r_0)^4

and hence

  Reg_nu(nu*r*omega).

The L^2 denominator is essential.  Under critical spatial rescaling, the
strong lifespan and ||omega||_2^4 transform with the same parabolic factor.
Thus Q is the scale-balanced quantity; the bare lifespan is not.


4. THE RADIAL MINIMUM
---------------------

Define the true sphere minimum

  M_*(r)

  := inf_{omega in Sigma} Q_*(r,omega).

Define the certified sphere minimum

  M(r)

  := inf_{omega in Sigma} Q_lower(r,omega).

Since Q_lower <= Q_*,

  M(r) <= M_*(r).

The most restricting normalized boundary datum at radius r is any minimizer

  omega_r^star in argmin_{omega in Sigma} Q_lower(r,omega).

If the minimum is not attained, use a minimizing sequence

  omega_{r,n}^star in Sigma,

  Q_lower(r,omega_{r,n}^star) -> M(r).

The associated physical boundary data are

  b_r^star := nu*r*omega_r^star.

This is the exact replacement for the informal "angle":

  b_r^star is the point on the H^(1/2) sphere that has the smallest certified
  local-window / QFB-overlap ratio.


5. REQUIRED INHERITED RADIUS AND RADIAL RESERVE
-----------------------------------------------

For M(r) > 0 define the required inherited normalized radius

  B(r)

  := r * M(r)^(-1/4).

Indeed,

  M(r) > (r/r_0)^4

is equivalent to

  B(r) < r_0.

Thus B(r) is the smallest already-admitted radius that is sufficient to absorb
the entire sphere of radius r through the certified overlap mechanism.

Define the self-reserve

  D(r)

  := r - B(r)

   = r * (1 - M(r)^(-1/4)).

Then

  D(r) > 0

is equivalent to

  M(r) > 1.

Interpretation:

  M(r) > 1

means that the sphere of radius r can be absorbed using a radius strictly
smaller than r.

The critical obstruction is

  M(r) = 1,

or equivalently

  B(r) = r,

or equivalently

  D(r) = 0.

7. FIRST-CONTACT PRINCIPLE
--------------------------

Assume:

  (FC1) ADMISSION_RADIUS_nu(r_base).

  (FC2) M(r) > 1 for every finite r >= r_base.

  (FC3) M is lower semicontinuous in r.

Then

  ADMISSION_RADIUS_nu(r)

holds for every finite r >= r_base.

Derivation:

Let r_max be the supremum of the connected interval of admitted radii starting
at r_base.

If r_max were finite, then every datum with

  ||b||_X < nu*r_max

would lie in an already-admitted smaller ball.  Hence

  ADMISSION_RADIUS_nu(r_max)

would hold.

By FC2,

  M(r_max) > 1.

By FC3, there exists delta > 0 such that for every

  r in [r_max,r_max+delta],

  M(r) > (r/r_max)^4.

The shell-absorption theorem then gives

  ADMISSION_RADIUS_nu(r_max+delta),

contradicting maximality.

Therefore

  r_max = infinity.

Consequently

  I(r) = r^2/(2*pi) -> infinity.

Under the frozen H12_sigma_0-to-NSEB adapter,

  forall r, ADMISSION_RADIUS_nu(r)

implies

  NSEB_nu.

  ->

8. DIFFERENTIAL PROPAGATION OF THE MINIMUM
------------------------------------------

The condition

  M(r) > 1 for every finite r

can be reduced to a radial differential inequality.

Assume:

  (DP1) M is locally absolutely continuous on [r_base,infinity).

  (DP2) M(r_base) > 1.

  (DP3) There exists a nonnegative locally integrable function a(r) such that

        M'(r) >= -a(r)*(M(r)-1)

        for almost every r >= r_base.

Then Gronwall gives

  M(r)-1

  >=

  (M(r_base)-1)
  * exp(-integral_{r_base}^r a(s) ds)

  > 0

for every finite r.

Therefore

  M(r) > 1

for every finite r, even if

  M(r) -> 1

as r -> infinity.

Thus local integrability of a is sufficient for arbitrary finite-radius
continuation.  Global integrability of a is only needed for a uniform positive
asymptotic gap.

Frozen differential endpoint:

  RADIAL_MINIMUM_GAP_PROPAGATION:

  M(r_base) > 1
  + M'(r) >= -a(r)*(M(r)-1)
  + a in L^1_loc

  ->

  forall finite r >= r_base, M(r) > 1.

Combined with Section 7:

  RADIAL_MINIMUM_GAP_PROPAGATION
  + LOWER_SEMICONTINUITY(M)
  + ADMISSION_RADIUS_nu(r_base)

  ->

  ->

9. ACTIVE MINIMIZER AND ENVELOPE DERIVATIVE
-------------------------------------------

In a finite-dimensional Galerkin or profile-compactified model, assume:

  Sigma_N is compact,

  Q_lower_N(r,omega) is continuous in (r,omega),

  Q_lower_N is differentiable in r.

Define

  M_N(r)

  := min_{omega in Sigma_N} Q_lower_N(r,omega),

and the active minimizer set

  A_N(r)

  := argmin_{omega in Sigma_N} Q_lower_N(r,omega).

Under the standard envelope hypotheses, the right radial derivative is

  D_plus M_N(r)

  = min_{omega in A_N(r)} partial_r Q_lower_N(r,omega).

Therefore the datum most resistant to radial continuation is selected in two
stages:

  Stage 1:

    minimize Q_lower_N(r,omega) over omega.

  Stage 2:

    among active minimizers, minimize partial_r Q_lower_N(r,omega).

Define

  RESISTOR_N(r)

  := argmin_{omega in A_N(r)} partial_r Q_lower_N(r,omega).

It is sufficient to certify

  partial_r Q_lower_N(r,omega)

  >=

  -a(r)*(Q_lower_N(r,omega)-1)

for every

  omega in A_N(r).

Then

  D_plus M_N(r)

  >=

  -a(r)*(M_N(r)-1).

This is the exact radial derivative/minimum object requested by the program.


10. LIFESPAN-EXCESS FORM
------------------------

Recall

  Q_lower(r,omega)

  = 2*S_lower(r,omega)/||omega||_2^4.

Define the overlap excess

  E(r,omega)

  := 2*S_lower(r,omega) - ||omega||_2^4.

Then

  Q_lower(r,omega)-1

  = E(r,omega)/||omega||_2^4.

Because the denominator is independent of r,

  partial_r Q_lower(r,omega)

  = 2*partial_r S_lower(r,omega)/||omega||_2^4.

Hence the active-minimizer differential inequality is equivalent to

  partial_r S_lower(r,omega)

  >=

  -a(r)*(S_lower(r,omega) - (1/2)*||omega||_2^4),

or equivalently

  partial_r E(r,omega)

  >=

  -a(r)*E(r,omega).

Thus the exact analytic target is:

  the positive lifespan excess above the QFB overlap threshold cannot decay
  faster than proportionally to itself along every active worst profile.

This formulation allows the minimizing profile to switch with r.  No single
fixed direction is assumed to remain extremal.


11. FIRST-CONTACT RESISTOR SYSTEM
--------------------------------

Suppose, contrary to the target, that M reaches 1 at a first finite radius
r_star:

  M(r) > 1 for r_base <= r < r_star,

  M(r_star) = 1.

Under compactness and differentiability, there exists an active first-contact
profile omega_star satisfying

  ||omega_star||_X = 1,

  Q_lower(r_star,omega_star) = 1,

  D_omega Q_lower(r_star,omega_star)
  is normal to Sigma,

and the relevant one-sided radial envelope derivative is nonpositive.

For a smooth constrained minimizer this gives the system

  (C1) ||omega_star||_X = 1,

  (C2) 2*S_lower(r_star,omega_star) = ||omega_star||_2^4,

  (C3) D_omega Q_lower(r_star,omega_star)
       = lambda * D_omega(||omega_star||_X^2),

  (C4) partial_r Q_lower(r_star,omega_star) <= 0.

Call this system

  RADIAL_FIRST_CONTACT_RESISTOR.

Therefore an alternative closure route is:

  PROFILE_COMPACTNESS
  + NONEXISTENCE_OF_RADIAL_FIRST_CONTACT_RESISTOR

  ->

  M(r) never reaches 1 at finite r

  ->

  I -> infinity.

This system is the exact nonlinear extremal problem to search computationally.


12. STATIONARITY EQUATION FOR THE PROFILE
----------------------------------------

Let

  L2MASS(omega) := ||omega||_2^2.

Since

  Q_lower(r,omega)

  = 2*S_lower(r,omega)/L2MASS(omega)^2,

a finite-dimensional smooth minimizer satisfies, for every variation h,

  D_omega S_lower(r,omega)[h] / S_lower(r,omega)

  -

  4*<omega,h>_L2 / ||omega||_2^2

  2*lambda*<omega,h>_X.

For tangent variations

  <omega,h>_X = 0,

this reduces to

  D_omega S_lower(r,omega)[h] / S_lower(r,omega)

  =

  4*<omega,h>_L2 / ||omega||_2^2.

This equation identifies the candidate profile rather than merely evaluating a
constant over a preselected family.

A positive constrained Hessian isolates a locally unique active branch.
Branch switching must be handled by retaining the full active minimizer set.

14. INFINITE-DIMENSIONAL NONCOMPACTNESS
---------------------------------------

For a finite Galerkin sphere Sigma_N, a minimizer exists.  However,

  min_{omega in Sigma_N} Q_lower(r,omega)

is not by itself a lower bound for the full infinite-dimensional minimum.
The finite-dimensional minimum can be larger than the true minimum.

A sound transfer requires an explicit tail/profile defect epsilon_N(r) such
that

  M(r) >= M_N(r) - epsilon_N(r).

Define the certified full-space lower envelope

  M_hat_N(r)

  := M_N(r) - epsilon_N(r).

Then

  M_hat_N(r) > 1

implies

  M(r) > 1.

The required infinite-dimensional endpoint is one of:

  RADIAL_PROFILE_COMPACTIFICATION,

  SCALE_PACKET_REDUCTION,

  TAIL_PERMEABILITY_LOWER_BOUND,

or an equivalent theorem yielding

  M(r) >= M_N(r)-epsilon_N(r).

Because Q is scale-balanced, high-frequency concentration cannot be discarded
merely from the smallness of ||omega||_2.  Lifespan and L^2 mass carry matching
parabolic scaling.  Scale or profile normalization is therefore necessary.

16. EXACT OPEN ENDPOINTS
------------------------

The B-tier reduction isolates the following nonformal obligations:

  O1. Construct a rigorous strong-window functional S_lower(r,omega).

  O2. Identify or enclose all active minimizers of Q_lower at each radius.

  O3. Prove a full-space profile/tail lower bound for M.

existence estimates generally produce a lifespan that decreases with amplitude
and therefore need not preserve the overlap gap.  The intended certificate
must exploit profile structure, a posteriori evolution, cancellation, or a
bootstrapped global background, rather than only a sharper universal Sobolev
constant.
