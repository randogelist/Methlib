MATHEMATICAL CONTENT
--------------------

For every positive viscosity and every smooth periodic divergence-free,
mean-zero L2 datum, the overlap axiom supplies one Leray-Hopf restart time
s, one positive lag eta and one local classical endpoint T such that

  s + eta < T

and

  cutoff(u(s),nu) < eta.

The restarted trajectory is an actual Leray-Hopf solution from its trace.
The established QFB theorem therefore supplies a classical tail for the
restart at eta. The overlap certificate's final field is the formal endpoint
of local weak-strong identification and smooth patching, producing a
classical tail from time zero.
