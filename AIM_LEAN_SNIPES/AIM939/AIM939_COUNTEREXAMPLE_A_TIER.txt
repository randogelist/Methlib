2. EXPLICIT COUNTEREXAMPLE

n=p=4.
a=(1,2,3,5,1).

f(x)=1+2 sigma_1(x)+3 sigma_2(x)+5 sigma_3(x)+sigma_4(x),
where sigma_k are the ordinary unnormalized elementary symmetric sums.
Core.lean explicitly enumerates every term in four variables.

P(t)=f(t,t,t,t)=1+8t+18t^2+20t^3+t^4.

The verified diagonal branch uses
  P'(t)=8+36t+60t^2+4t^3,
  P''(t)=36+120t+12t^2,
  W(t)=1+2t+6t^2+26t^3+22t^4,
  4P(t)P''(t)-3P'(t)^2=-48W(t).

For t>0, P(t)>0 and W(t)>0. The second derivative of P^(1/4) is
  -3 W(t) P(t)^(-7/4),
which is nonpositive. The compiled theorem diagonal_concave applies on
the entire interval (0,infinity).

The interior midpoint witness is
  xMinus=(1/200,397/100,1/200,1/200),
  xMid=(1/100,4,1/100,1/100),
  xPlus=(3/200,403/100,3/200,3/200),
with xMid=(xMinus+xPlus)/2.

Exact values:
  f(xMinus)=7320291897/800000000,
  f(xMid)=9426909/1000000,
  f(xPlus)=7767765381/800000000.

Let
  qMinus=17392403/10000000,
  qMid=17522350/10000000,
  qPlus=17652309/10000000.

Compiled rational comparisons give
  qMinus^4 < f(xMinus),
  f(xMid) < qMid^4,
  qPlus^4 < f(xPlus),
  (qMinus+qPlus)/2 - qMid = 3/5000000 > 0.

Fourth-root monotonicity and cancellation yield
  f(xMid)^(1/4)
    < qMid
    < (qMinus+qPlus)/2
    < [f(xMinus)^(1/4)+f(xPlus)^(1/4)]/2.

Therefore midpoint concavity fails at interior points.

The final theorem specializes an assumed universal coefficient assertion
to this explicit vector and contradicts the certified midpoint failure.
