CERTIFIED_ACCEPTED_SET_PRESERVATION

  CertifiedDodgsonProjection K includes a proved implication:
    original acceptance -> AllValid(projected finite cells).

  Under that fixed projection correctness proof:
    prunedCheck(c)=originalCheck(c),
    prunedAcceptance(c)<->originalAcceptance(c),
    existence of pruned acceptance <-> existence of original acceptance,
    verified-byte acceptance is unchanged.

  An uncertified filter can discard an accepted certificate; its RH soundness
  does not imply accepted-set preservation.

DODGSON_CELL_SCOPE

  Finite rational Cell data are checked through a Turan numerator.
  Strict cell validity implies positive Turan numerator.
  A failed necessary test excludes that strict cell class.
  Geometric triples have zero Turan numerator and are excluded from that class.
  A proved uniform nonpositive Turan upper bound excludes the represented
  parameter region. Semidefinite unregularized zero determinants are not
  excluded merely by these strict-cell claims.
