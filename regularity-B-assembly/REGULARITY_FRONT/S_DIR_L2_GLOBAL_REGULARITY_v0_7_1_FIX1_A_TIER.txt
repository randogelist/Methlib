THEOREM SCOPE
  For every nonzero direction a : Z^3 and every complex coefficient
  function z on the nonzero plane {k:a.k=0, k!=0} with
  sum_k |z_k|^2 < infinity:

  Sum over pairs (k,l) of
    |z_k| |z_l| / (|k|^2+|l|^2)
  is absolutely summable and bounded above by
    64 * sum_k |z_k|^2.

  assert physical C-infinity regularity, the physical L2_t L-infinity_x
