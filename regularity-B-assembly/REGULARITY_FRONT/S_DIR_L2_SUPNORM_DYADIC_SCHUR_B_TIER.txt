Let a be any nonzero integer vector in Z^3 and let
  Lambda_a={k in Z^3:a.k=0}.
Let R(k)=(k_1^2+k_2^2+k_3^2)^(1/2) and
  Q(k)=max(|k_1|,|k_2|,|k_3|).
For k != 0, Q(k) is a positive integer and Q(k)<=R(k).

Define j(k)=floor(log_2 Q(k)), so
  2^j(k) <= Q(k) < 2^(j(k)+1).
Consequently, S_j={k in Lambda_a\{0}:j(k)=j} form a genuine
pairwise-disjoint partition. Their finite intersections with any F
form a disjoint finite partition of F, without an admission.

The previously derived injective projection of Lambda_a onto two integer
coordinates gives, in the cube |k_i|<2^(j+1),
  #S_j <= (2^(j+2)-1)^2 <= 16*4^j.

For a fixed r>0, set h_r(k)=1/[R(k)*(r^2+R(k)^2)].
On S_j, R(k)>=Q(k)>=2^j, and the map s -> 1/[s(r^2+s^2)]
is decreasing on s>0. Thus, for arbitrary finite F,

  sum_{k in F cap S_j} h_r(k)
     <= 16 * 4^j / [2^j*(r^2+4^j)]
     = 16 * 2^j/(r^2+4^j).

The already proved telescopic dyadic estimate is

  sum_{j=0}^{N-1} 2^j/(r^2+4^j) <= 4/r.

Using the actual disjoint shell partition of every finite subset
F subset Lambda_a\{0}, one gets

  sum_{k in F} h_r(k) <=64/r.

Because h_r>=0 and all finite subsums have the same uniform bound,
the series is summable and

  sum_{k in Lambda_a\{0}} 1/[R(k)*(r^2+R(k)^2)] <=64/r.

This is an unconditional constant, uniform in the nonzero direction a,
without primitive-axis assumptions. The external Lean target encodes
the series over all k in Z^3 with zero extension off Lambda_a\{0}.

The physical Serrin estimate still requires Fourier-torus synthesis and
the exact time-integrated heat kernel, and unrestricted Leray-Hopf
uniqueness requires a separate weak-strong uniqueness theorem.
