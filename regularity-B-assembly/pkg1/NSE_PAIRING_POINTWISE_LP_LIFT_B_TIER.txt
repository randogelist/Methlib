NSE_PAIRING_POINTWISE_LP_LIFT_B_TIER
====================================

MODE=B_TIER_DERIVATION
DATE=2026-11-10
THEOREM=NSE_PAIRING_POINTWISE_LP_LIFT
MARKER=!

Let q belong to L^1(mu), and let f,g,h belong to L^3(mu). Let

  T := tripleProduct(f,g,h),

where T belongs to L^(pFinal)(mu) and pFinal=1.

Assume

  |q(x)| <= |T(x)|

for almost every x.

By monotonicity of the L^1 norm,

  ||q||_L1 <= ||T||_L1.

Because pFinal=1, the right-hand side is precisely the norm of the existing
Holder product:

  ||T||_L1 = ||tripleProduct(f,g,h)||.

Hence

  ||q||_L1 <= ||tripleProduct(f,g,h)||.

This proves the field

  NSEPairingRealization.pointwiseLift

from a pointwise almost-everywhere inequality.

Consequently:

  PointwiseLpData
    -> NSEPairingRealization
    -> CriticalHolder.

Together with PeriodicHhalfL3:

  Iabs <= C3^3 E D^2,

and if E <= nu/(4 C3^3),

  Iabs <= (nu/4)D^2.

The remaining semantic obligation is no longer a norm inequality. It is only
the pointwise representative identity/domination for the concrete periodic
NSE fields.
