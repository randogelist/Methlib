CONTRACT REVIEW

Contracts.lean states the actual unit-periodic, zero-force NSE using Mathlib
derivatives, closed-half-space C-infinity regularity, the initial trace,
divergence freedom, velocity mean zero, and normalized pressure. No arbitrary
regularity predicate or unspecified solution operator was found in this contract.

Admission.lean uses extended nonnegative integrals, so divergent enstrophy
cannot become zero through Bochner-integral totalization. AX6Statement quantifies
over global GalerkinSolution witnesses. Until endpoint 3 proves these witnesses
exist for every admissible input and cutoff, an AX6 instance alone cannot select
a trajectory. The separate existence obligation is essential, not additional
axiomatic permission.

GalerkinSolution.coeff is defined on all real times but constrained only for
t >= 0. Uniqueness must therefore mean equality on nonnegative times (or use
an explicitly fixed negative-time extension). An unrestricted unique-inhabitant
claim for the structure itself would be false because negative-time values
remain arbitrary. The physical forward-trajectory uniqueness target is unchanged.

UNRESOLVED DOWNSTREAM EDGES

7. AX6_UNIFORM_STRONG_BOUNDS
Required actual identifications: sixEnstrophy equals physical enstrophy;
the latter is finite and continuous for finite synthesis; conversion of the
extended square budget to the real interval-integral budget; physical enstrophy
and dissipation differential inequality, uniformly in N; initial H1 cutoff
contraction. The scalar bound then follows from endpoint 6. An L2_t L2_x bound
for the actual time derivative additionally needs the Leray-projection L2
contraction and the periodic Sobolev/product estimate for the convection term.
UniformBounds.lean proves the cutoff-independent scalar accumulation step only.
It deliberately does not use the frozen endpoint-7 theorem name.

8. GALERKIN_GLOBAL_STRONG_LIMIT
Required: one subsequence/limit compatible on all finite horizons; weak and
strong convergence in the necessary spaces; identification of the nonlinear
limit with physical convection; recovery of the initial datum; inheritance of
the H1/H2 bounds. Weak global Leray--Hopf existence by itself does not supply
the strong-solution conclusion.

9. GLOBAL_STRONG_SMOOTH_BOOTSTRAP
Required: actual spatial/time derivative regularity from H1/H2 strong bounds;
arbitrarily high orders for smooth input; regularity through t=0 rather than
only on positive-time compact subintervals. No concrete theorem with these
conclusions was found among the inspected upstream files.

10. PERIODIC_PRESSURE_RECOVERY
Required: construction of the mean-zero periodic inverse-Laplacian pressure,
convergence and differentiation of its Fourier series, joint smoothness,
and recovery of the literal momentum equation. A formal coefficient formula
without these analytic properties does not discharge this endpoint.

11. ax6_implies_fefferman_B_0_adjust
The exact target type must contain only AX6Statement as the unresolved analytic
premise. A theorem taking GlobalStrong, GlobalPositiveFlow, SeedExtension,
compactness, continuation, pressure-recovery, or endpoint-record arguments
does not meet this scope even if its axiom report lists standard axioms only.
