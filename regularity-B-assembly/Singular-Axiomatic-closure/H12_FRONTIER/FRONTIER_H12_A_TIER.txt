1. EXACT CONSTANT SCOPE
----------------------
The constants CSHalf, CSOne and CSZero are fixed conservative admissible
constants, not optimal or independently prescribed numerical constants.
Their actual finite-Fourier Sobolev inequalities and ordinary Lebesgue
normalization on the dilated 2*pi cube are proved in LiteratureConstants.lean.

K2 = sqrt(2)*CSHalf*CSOne*CSZero/(2*pi).
CF = K2/(2*sqrt(pi)) = (2*pi)^3*nativeProduct > 0.
The finite nonlinear estimate is proved with CF. frontierCertificate is
constructed, not a hypothesis of either concrete root.

For mean-zero data:
  nativeNorm(a) = sqrt(2*pi)*paperNorm(1,a).
Thus the public smallness condition is exactly equivalent to the native one.
The strict radius identity nu/K2 < sqrt(2)*nu/K2 is separately audited for the
same fixed K2. There is no claim of optimality or historical priority.
