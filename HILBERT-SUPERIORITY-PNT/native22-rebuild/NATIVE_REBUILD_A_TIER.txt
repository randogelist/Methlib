MAIN OPEN SCOPES:
- Actual von Mangoldt/zeta log derivative: exact identity obtained as an
  implication assuming PrimePowerReindexing AND EulerProductLogDerivative;
  neither is proved in this package. A tsum alone does not prove convergence.
- Zeta nonvanishing for Re(s)>1: derived conditionally from an actual
  zeta-Moebius L-series inverse identity, which is not proved here.
- Riemann-Lebesgue: real-frequency helper with explicit half-period phase
  cancellation and L1 translation-limit assumptions; neither is discharged.
- Mellin: finite Abel and finite complex-power identities, with differentiability
  and integrability premises; the infinite limit and coefficient adapter are open.
- Fubini: separable exchange and fixed-frequency algebra, not the full coupled
  continuous-frequency swap.
- Dominated-limit helper assumes a weighted-uniform-error bound and integrability;
  it is not the general dominated convergence theorem.
- Nonnegative sum-integral interchange requires integrability of the total
  series, almost-everywhere pointwise summability, and summable mode integrals.
- Fourier convolution is the real-line complex-valued specialization and uses a
  Mathlib convolution-integral helper that itself uses Mathlib Fubini.
- The l2 inner identity uses Mathlib's existing Hilbert-space inner product.
- Zeta analyticity uses Mathlib regularized-zeta differentiability, whose
  foundation is not independently reconstructed by this package.
