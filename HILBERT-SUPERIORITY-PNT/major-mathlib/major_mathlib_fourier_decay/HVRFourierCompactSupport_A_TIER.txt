HVR Fourier compact support update: A_TIER_CANDIDATE until the new branch CI compiles and audits matching sources.
The new files formalize half-period cancellation, compact-support L1 translation continuity, a filter-generic squeeze, and the ordinary frequency conversion. These are scoped supporting declarations, not yet a compiled unconditional composite or PNT root replacement.
Proofs in HVRFourierPhase and HVRCompactTranslation adapt Mathlib.Analysis.Fourier.RiemannLebesgueLemma (Copyright 2022 David Loeffler; Apache 2.0), using lower-level imports; no claim of novel independent discovery or verified target independence. Existing Mathlib analytic and integration infrastructure remains.
