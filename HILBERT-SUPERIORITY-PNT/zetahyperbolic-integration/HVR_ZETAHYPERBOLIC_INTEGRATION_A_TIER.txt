This package contains a source-level PNT root integration candidate plus
separate finite-dimensional and conditional interfaces:
1. HVR_PrimeCount_MellinCandidate preserves both original theorem signatures
   while calling the refactored prime-mode assembly. The final root reaches
   that exact mode theorem according to the positive-controlled proof trace.
   HVR_PrimeMellin_Abel remains an independently audited historical helper;
   it adapts a pinned Mathlib proof by Xavier Roblot (Apache 2.0). The trace
   did not see that helper or LSeries_eq_mul_integral in the mode root, with
   negative coverage unverified.
2. HVR_PrimeMellin_Modes contains actual prime mode calculations and a
   conditional theta exponential integral assembly. The separate downstream
   HVR_PrimeMellin_ModeAssembly discharges its a.e. theta expansion and
   applies the existing change of variables to obtain the Mellin identity.
   Definitions and conversion lemmas have been factored upstream to avoid
   the former import cycle, and the root now uses this assembly. It still
   uses Mathlib integral_tsum, Jacobian and other analysis infrastructure.
3. HVRPrimeAtomicGram and HVRPrimeGramJacobiBridge prove finite-cutoff prime
   Gram identities and connect them to the adapted Jacobi matrix. Strict
   positivity is proved only with an explicit nonzero prime-witness premise.
4. HVR_PrimeMomentJacobi composes actual finite HVR prime moments with a port
   of Zetahyperbolic finite matrix/Jacobi algebra. Its general log-determinant
   theorem has an explicit determinant-nonzero hypothesis.
5. HVRZetaHyperbolicFiniteFrequency connects the fixed-frequency Fubini
   identity to the actual prime-density transform and finite frequency sums.
   The continuous-frequency equality is conditional on TWO convergence
   hypotheses. It does not replace the PNT root's continuous swap.
6. HVRZetaHyperbolicFrequencyBounds proves the phase and weighted-integral
   Lipschitz estimates under explicit integrability and moment hypotheses.
   HVRPrimeWeightedMoment establishes the actual damped prime-error moment
   for a positive damping parameter. HVRPrimeLaplaceFrequencyBounds establishes
   actual frequency Lipschitz control. HVRPrimeWeightedFrequencyApproximation
   gives a weighted finite-frequency adapter. Its continuous identity still
   assumes both approximation limits; they remain open. HVRFrequencyCellError
   adds a one-cell bound, and HVRFrequencyFiniteCells aggregates it over a
   given finite family. Cell integrability, mesh and budget hypotheses remain.
