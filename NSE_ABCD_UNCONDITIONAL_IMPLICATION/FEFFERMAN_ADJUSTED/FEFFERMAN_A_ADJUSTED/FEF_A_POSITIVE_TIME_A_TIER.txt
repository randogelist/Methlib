PRIMARY THEOREM
  Fefferman.ConcreteReal.AdjustedA.FEF_A_POSITIVE_TIME
  ∀ ε, Fefferman_adjusted_A ε.
  AdjustedFeffermanA is a compatibility abbreviation for Fefferman_adjusted_A.

Unfolded:
  ∀ ε ν u₀, admissibleInitial .wholeSpace u₀ →
  PositiveTimeState ε ν u₀ →
  hasGlobalSolution .wholeSpace ν u₀ zeroForce.

PositiveTimeState gives a Schwartz solenoidal seed b, its L³ smallness
relative to εν, an age τ>0 and an exhibited flow w with u₀=w(τ).
GlobalPositiveFlow requires that the earlier fields solve the unforced
equation, have seed b and uniformly bounded kinetic energy, and have
analytic velocity and pressure at strictly positive times. At its seed
the velocity is C∞. Its pressure need only be analytic at positive times.
The seed norm uses the Euclidean vector length. Its cube is integrable
and satisfies ∫ |b|³ < (εν)³.

The constructive witness is w(x,t+τ), with the pressure translated by
the same amount. ShiftAnalytic.lean proves that the closed restarted
half-space is mapped inside the open analytic region. ShiftCore.lean
translates the original momentum equation, divergence, initial condition
and uniform energy, preserving its bound. No assumption of Fefferman A,
global solution at u₀, Diamond realization, or abstract regularity exit
is a proof parameter of the primary theorem. The proof uses a global
ancestor supplied by the initial-state class.

FINITE-HISTORY VARIANT
  regularity_of_seed_extension
  ∀ ε, SeedExtension ε → ∀ ν u₀,
  admissibleInitial .wholeSpace u₀ → FinitePositiveTimeState ε ν u₀ →
  hasGlobalSolution .wholeSpace ν u₀ zeroForce.

FiniteHistory specifies the actual PDE and bounded energy on [0,τ];
there is no future continuation in this input. SeedExtension specifies
existence of a small-data global flow and equality of its snapshot with
any such finite history. The B-tier file derives existence of a positive
threshold ε* using the published PDE results. The Lean module does not
prove ∃ ε*>0, SeedExtension ε*. It exposes the premise in the finite-history
theorem. No numerical value is assigned to the universal threshold.

NONEMPTY CLASS
  zero_is_positive_time_state supplies an actual seed, flow and age τ=1
  at every ε>0 and ν>0. ZeroSeed.admissible_zero supplies original
  admissibility, and ZeroSeed.zero_global constructs the physical solution.
  The explicit example is zero. The primary theorem applies to every
  admitted member of its restricted class.
