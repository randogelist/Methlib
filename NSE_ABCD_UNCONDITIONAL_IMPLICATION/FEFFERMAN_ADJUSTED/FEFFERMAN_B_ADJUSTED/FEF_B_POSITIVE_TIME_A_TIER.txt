TYPE=∀ ε : ℝ, Fefferman_adjusted_B ε

THEOREM
For every positive viscosity ν, admissible periodic u₀, and exhibited
positive-time state u₀ of a small periodic unforced flow at age τ>0,
there exists s with globalSolution realAnalysis .periodic ν u₀ zeroForce s.

INPUT CLASS
The smallness condition is per unit spatial cell: the integral of |b|³
is finite and strictly less than (εν)³. The seed is smooth, solenoidal
and unit-periodic. The exhibited ancestor has periodic velocity and
pressure, satisfies the unforced PDE, has trace b, and is jointly
analytic for strictly positive times. PositiveTimeState identifies
u₀ with its velocity at τ. The original admissibility of u₀ is retained.

WITNESS
Set u(x,t)=w(x,t+τ), p(x,t)=q(x,t+τ). At t=0 the trace is u₀.
Time translation preserves momentum (01), divergence (02), and
the new trace (03). It preserves unit-periodicity of velocity (10)
and pressure (pressure erratum). Since t≥0 implies t+τ>0, the translated
velocity and pressure satisfy the literal analytic regularity clause (11),
including the new initial time. These are exactly the v0.25.0 periodic
solution clauses.

SCOPE
The root constructs the shifted witness for the exhibited-flow class.
Construction of a global ancestor for arbitrary small periodic seeds
and its agreement with a given finite history is the separate input
SeedExtension ε. regularity_of_seed_extension names that input explicitly.
Universal Fefferman B remains outside the proved scope of this restriction.

EXPLICIT NONZERO INSTANCE
For ε>0 and ν>0 take c=εν/2, b(x)=(c,0,0), w(x,t)=b(x), q(x,t)=0,
and τ=1. The cube integral over the unit cell is c³ < (εν)³. The theorem
ConstantSeed.exists_nonzero_positive_time_state supplies admissibility,
class membership and global existence for this nonzero datum.

VERIFICATION
Run run.cmd on native Windows with the installed Lean 4.33.0 toolchain
and cached Mathlib. The runner checks the exact periodic type, prints
the 16 theorem-level axiom reports and records version.txt, axioms.log and
result.txt in the run's evidence directory. Promotion requires the
matching external RUN=PASS with AXIOMS=STANDARD_ONLY. Allowed axioms
are propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound or subsets.
