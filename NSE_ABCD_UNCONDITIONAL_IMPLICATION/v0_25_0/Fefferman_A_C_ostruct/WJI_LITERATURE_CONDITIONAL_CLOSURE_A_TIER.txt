SOLE LITERATURE HYPOTHESIS
For every c, s, T>0, if SmoothFiniteEnergySlab c T s holds and every component
of datum c has a Schwartz representative, then for every time order n and
finite spatial order k there is C : ENNReal with C < infinity such that,
for every component i and spatial word w with length(w)<=k, almost every
t in (0,T) satisfies
  eLpNorm (wjiPositiveTimeWord s n i w t) 2 volume
  + eLpNorm (wjiPositiveTimeWord s n i w t) infinity volume <= C.
The new source displays the exact Lean type. The fresh root audit prints it.
No jet equality, jet-polynomial growth, analytic obstruction, or endpoint
failure is included in this hypothesis. It is a derived literature corollary,
not a verbatim paper theorem and not an imported Lean proof of Tao's results.

LITERATURE DERIVATION OF THE ADMITTED COROLLARY
Reference: Terence Tao, Analysis & PDE 6 (2013), 25-107,
doi:10.2140/apde.2013.6.25.
https://msp.org/apde/2013/6-1/apde-v6-n1-p02-s.pdf
1. Schwartz initial data and zero force give H1 data.
2. Corollary 11.1 upgrades the smooth finite-energy slab to an H1 solution.
3. Corollary 4.3 produces a mild H1 solution with the same velocity after
   pressure normalization.
4. Theorem 5.4(iv) gives every time derivative in L-infinity_t H^m_x on
   [0,T], for every m, including the initial-time endpoint.
5. For spatial order k use m=k+2. Sobolev embedding in dimension three and
   the finitely many components and words of length at most k give a finite
   common L2-plus-L-infinity bound. Convert that bound to ENNReal.
Steps 1-5 and the correspondence with the literal slab definition are outside
the new Lean proof. Their combined conclusion is the explicit hypothesis.
