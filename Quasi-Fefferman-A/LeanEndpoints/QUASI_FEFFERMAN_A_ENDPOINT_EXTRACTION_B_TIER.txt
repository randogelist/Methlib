  EP114: square the nonnegative sides.

  Output: 4*pi^2*A^4<=D*Z.

LE016-LE017 / D06.08:

    N^4<=A^4*D^2*Z^2.

  Each interval (0,min(T,1/(n+1))] has positive Lebesgue measure.
  EP094 supplies epsilon_n in that interval satisfying P.
  The squeeze 0<epsilon_n<=1/(n+1) gives epsilon_n->0.
  No monotonicity of epsilon is asserted or required.

LE027 / D09.04, differential comparison:
  On [s,T], y and a are continuous, y'=d on (s,T), and d<=a*y.
  Set A(t)=integral_s^t a and H(t)=y(t)*exp(-A(t)).
  H'(t)=(d(t)-a(t)y(t))*exp(-A(t))<=0.
  H is nonincreasing, so
    y(t)<=y(s)*exp(integral_s^t a).
  Neither sign(a) nor derivatives at the endpoints are required.

LE028 / D09.04, discontinuous integral energy:
  a is continuous and nonnegative on [s,T]; a*e is interval integrable;
  e(t)<=q+integral_s^t a*e at every t in [s,T].
  Let F(t)=q+integral_s^t a*e and G(t)=q+integral_s^t a*F.
  F and G are continuous; e<=F and a>=0 imply F<=G.
  On (s,T), G'=a*F<=a*G. LE027 gives
    e(t)<=F(t)<=G(t)<=q*exp(integral_s^t a).
  No continuity or derivative of e is assumed.

LE029 / D09.04, admissible trace:
  T>0; e>=0 on [0,T]; a continuous and nonnegative on (0,T];
  a and a*e interval integrable on [0,T]; integral_0^T a<=B;
  epsilon_n in (0,T], epsilon_n->0, e(epsilon_n)->0;
  e(t)<=e(epsilon_n)+integral_epsilon_n^t a*e
    for every t in [epsilon_n,T].
  For fixed t>0, eventually epsilon_n<t. LE028 and a>=0 give
    0<=e(t)<=e(epsilon_n)*exp(integral_epsilon_n^t a)
            <=e(epsilon_n)*exp(B)->0.
  Hence e(t)=0 for every t in (0,T].

LE030 / EP096 / D09.04, norm-valued comparison:
  Let u,v map real time into a normed additive commutative group and have
  the same strong trace b along epsilon_n. Set e(t)=||u(t)-v(t)||^2.
