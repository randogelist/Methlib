[01] EXACT ROOT STATEMENT

Omega = R^3, full Lebesgue measure; nu>0 constant; external force f=0.
H = L^2_sigma(R^3), the distributionally divergence-free real vector fields.
V = H intersect H^1(R^3;R^3).
M = ||a||_2.
T_QFA(a,nu) = M^4/(16*pi^2*nu^5).

LH_nu(a) is the class of velocity trajectories satisfying:
  u in C_w([0,infinity);H) intersect L^infinity_t L^2_x
       intersect L^2_t dot H^1_x;
  u(0)=a and strong right L^2 trace a;
  distributional unforced incompressible Navier-Stokes;
  strong energy inequality

    ||u(t)||_2^2 + 2*nu*integral_s^t ||grad u(r)||_2^2 dr <= ||u(s)||_2^2

  for s=0, for almost every s>0, and for every t>=s.

The velocity weak equation is tested against smooth compactly supported
solenoidal vector fields phi on [0,infinity) x R^3:

  integral integral [u.partial_t phi + (u tensor u):grad phi
                    - nu*grad u:grad phi] dx dt
    + integral a.phi(0) dx = 0.

Trajectory equality means equality in spatial L^2 at every time. Smoothness
means existence of a smooth spacetime representative of these L^2 classes.
Pressure is reconstructed separately; ungauged pressure pairs are not the
objects in the trajectory singleton below.

THEOREM QUASI_FEFFERMAN_A:

  For every nu>0 and a in H:

  (Q0) LH_nu(a) is nonempty.

  (Q1) For every u in LH_nu(a),
         lim_(t -> 0+) ||u(t)-a||_2 = 0.

  (Q2) For every u in LH_nu(a) and every delta>T_QFA(a,nu),
       there exist smooth representatives u,p on [delta,infinity) x R^3
       such that
         partial_t u + (u.grad)u + grad p = nu*Delta u,
         div u = 0.

  (Q3) For every such u and delta,
         LH_nu(u(delta)) = {tau |-> u(delta+tau)}.

Quantifier preservation:
  The admissible trajectory u is arbitrary.
  The restart witness s may depend on u and delta.
  Original-data uniqueness is neither assumed nor concluded.
  Actual nonuniqueness is neither assumed nor concluded.
  The frozen guarantee uses delta>T_QFA; equality is outside its scope.
  No smallness or mean-zero hypothesis on a is added.
  The numerical constant is sufficient; optimality is not asserted.

  APPLY [EP097] MeasureTheory.memLp_top_of_bound to the bounded component.
  APPLY [EP098] MeasureTheory.MemLp.mul with exponents infinity,2,2.
  OUTPUT: v_i*v_j in L2 at every positive regular time.

[D10.02] Bounded measurable Fourier multiplier.
  Set m_ij(k)=-k_i*k_j/|k|^2 for k!=0, and m_ij(0)=0.
  Rational operations give Borel measurability; |k_i|,|k_j|<=|k| give |m_ij|<=1.
  APPLY [EP097] MeasureTheory.memLp_top_of_bound with bound 1.
  APPLY [EP099] MeasureTheory.MemLp.smul with exponents infinity,2,2.
  APPLY [EP005] MeasureTheory.Lp.norm_le_norm_of_ae_le to |m_ij*h|<=|h|.
  SELECT [EP100] ContinuousLinearMap.holderL for scalar multiplication,
    measure=volume and exponents infinity,2,2, then fix the L-infinity symbol m.
  OUTPUT: an actual bounded linear L2 multiplication operator M_m.
  Conjugate M_m by the L2 Fourier isometry from [D06.02].
  OUTPUT: T_ij(f)=F0^(-1)(m_ij*F0(f)) is L2 contractive.
  Define p=sum_(i,j) T_ij(v_i*v_j).

[D10.03] Temperate multiplication on L2 distributions; derived bridge AD06.
  Let h and g*h belong to L2, and assume g.HasTemperateGrowth.
  This includes the polynomial derivative symbols and the Bessel weights.
  No global Lp hypothesis on g is imposed.
  APPLY [EP054] MeasureTheory.Lp.toTemperedDistribution_apply
    to h and g*h, testing with an arbitrary Schwartz phi.
  APPLY [EP055] TemperedDistribution.smulLeftCLM_apply_apply
    and [EP056] SchwartzMap.smulLeftCLM_apply_apply to the same test.
  Both sides evaluate to integral phi(k)*g(k)*h(k).
  OUTPUT: the tempered distribution of g*h equals g times that of h.

  For a bounded measurable m and such a g,
    g*(m*h)=m*(g*h) belongs to L2 by [D10.02].
  The multiplication identity, Fourier/distribution compatibility, and
    [EP060] MeasureTheory.Lp.ker_toTemperedDistributionCLM_eq_bot
  therefore give
    partial^alpha(T_m f)=T_m(partial^alpha f)
  whenever the physical derivatives in question belong to L2.
  This proves Hm preservation for the bounded multiplier.
  A global Holder assumption on polynomial g is not introduced.

[D10.04] Smooth pressure.
  For every integer m>=0, C03 gives v in H^(m+2) at positive times.
  Leibniz expresses each derivative of v_i*v_j through order m as a finite sum.
  Agmon [D06.06], applied to differentiated velocities, bounds one factor
  in L-infinity; the other factor is L2. APPLY
    [EP098] MeasureTheory.MemLp.mul termwise as in [D10.01].
  OUTPUT: v_i*v_j in Hm; AD06 gives p in Hm for every m.
  APPLY [EP101] TemperedDistribution.MemSobolev.fourier_memL1
    at sufficiently high order to each required derivative.
  More explicitly, every Fourier moment is integrable: for n>=0 use
    |k|^n*|phat| = (1+|k|^2)^(-1) * [(1+|k|^2)*|k|^n*|phat|].
  The second factor belongs to L2 by H^(n+2) membership.
  APPLY [EP102] integrable_rpow_neg_one_add_norm_sq with parameter r=4,
    so dimension 3<r and the theorem's power -r/2 equals -2.
  OUTPUT: (1+|k|^2)^(-2) is integrable.
  APPLY [EP069] MeasureTheory.memLp_two_iff_integrable_sq to conclude that
    (1+|k|^2)^(-1) belongs to L2.
  APPLY [EP070] MeasureTheory.MemLp.integrable_mul to these two L2 factors.
  Their product identity proves Integrable(k|->|k|^n*|phat(k)|).
  APPLY [EP071] MeasureTheory.integral_mul_le_Lp_mul_Lq_of_nonneg with p=q=2
    for the numerical bound, after this integrability has been established.
  APPLY [EP103] Real.contDiff_fourier with N=infinity, together with
    [EP104] Real.fourierInv_eq_fourier_comp_neg to handle the inverse.
  USE AD03 to identify this smooth representative with the pressure L2 class.
  C03 time regularity passes through the bounded linear multipliers and the
    bounded Sobolev-to-L-infinity embeddings supplied by Agmon. Leibniz
    products are bounded bilinear maps at sufficiently high Sobolev order.
  Consequently all mixed time-space derivatives are continuous; p is smooth
    on t>s. This argument is independent of the residual calculation below.

[D10.05] Poisson identity and real-valuedness.
  APPLY [EP070] MeasureTheory.MemLp.integrable_mul to the two L2 components:
    the real product f=v_i*v_j belongs to L1, and to L2 by [D10.01].
  APPLY [EP105] Real.fourierTransform_toLp to identify the ordinary forward
    integral with its bounded continuous L1 Fourier representative.
  The forward analogue of AD03 uses
    [EP106] TemperedDistribution.fourier_apply and
    [EP048] MeasureTheory.Lp.fourier_toTemperedDistribution_eq, and
    [EP107] SchwartzMap.fourier_coe
    in the same compact-test/Fubini proof; hence the ordinary and L2 Fourier
    representatives agree a.e. Conjugating the absolutely convergent forward
    integral gives h(-k)=conj(h(k)) for real f.
  The real even symbol m_ij preserves this identity. The high Sobolev
    estimates in [D10.04] make m_ij*h integrable.
  APPLY [EP108] MeasureTheory.integral_conj to the inverse integral,
    and [EP104] Real.fourierInv_eq_fourier_comp_neg with reflection k->-k.
  OUTPUT: the inverse pressure representative is real-valued.
  USE [D10.03] with g(k)=-(2*pi)^2*|k|^2, and the directional derivative
  identities from [D06.02]. Algebra gives
    -Delta p=sum_(i,j) partial_i partial_j(v_i*v_j).
  The L2 normalization excludes spatial additive constants.

[D10.06] Recovery of the full NSE; derived bridge AD07.
  At positive time let
    R=partial_t v+(v.grad)v-nu*Delta v+grad p.
  C03 and [D10.01]-[D10.03] give R in L2; higher positive-time Sobolev
  regularity is used for grad p.
  Solenoidal weak NSE gives integral R.phi=0 for every smooth compact
  solenoidal phi. The Poisson identity and div v=0 give div R=0.
  For arbitrary compact smooth scalar psi and i!=j, take
    phi_i=partial_j psi, phi_j=-partial_i psi, all other components zero.
  Then phi is solenoidal, and testing yields
    partial_j R_i-partial_i R_j=0 in distributions.
  APPLY [EP049] TemperedDistribution.fourier_lineDerivOp_eq to these identities.
  APPLY [EP052] MeasureTheory.LocallyIntegrable.continuous_smul to the
    polynomial frequency factors and the L2 Fourier representative Rhat.
  APPLY [EP109] ae_eq_zero_of_integral_contDiff_smul_eq_zero
    to their zero distributions. OUTPUT, almost everywhere,
    k_j*Rhat_i-k_i*Rhat_j=0 and sum_j k_j*Rhat_j=0.
  Multiplying the first identities by k_j and summing gives
    |k|^2*Rhat_i=0.
  Hence Rhat=0 off the null singleton {0}. APPLY
    [EP059] MeasureTheory.Lp.norm_fourier_eq to obtain R=0.
  Positive-time continuity upgrades a.e. time identities to every positive
  time. Thus the constructed pressure solves the full NSE. Poisson alone
  is not used as a sufficient condition for this conclusion.

[D10.07] Root assembly and quantifier preservation.

  USE [D10.01]-[D10.06]: smooth velocity and pressure representatives exist
