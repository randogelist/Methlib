[01] EXACT ROOT STATEMENT

H = L^2_sigma(R^3), the distributionally divergence-free real vector fields.

LH_nu(a) is the class of velocity trajectories satisfying:
  u in C_w([0,infinity);H) intersect L^infinity_t L^2_x
       intersect L^2_t dot H^1_x;
  u(0)=a and strong right L^2 trace a;
  distributional unforced incompressible Navier-Stokes;
  strong energy inequality

    ||u(t)||_2^2 + 2*nu*integral_s^t ||grad u(r)||_2^2 dr <= ||u(s)||_2^2

  for s=0, for almost every s>0, and for every t>=s.

means existence of a smooth spacetime representative of these L^2 classes.

  For R>0 and u in LH_nu(a),

    integral_0^R E(t)*D(t) dt
      <= (M^4-F(R)^2)/(4*nu)
      <= M^4/(4*nu),

Proof:
  The energy inequality gives 0<=E(t)<=F(t)<=M^2.
  D is nonnegative and integrable on each finite interval.
  Therefore the primitive of D is absolutely continuous, and so is F.
  Almost everywhere, F'(t)=-2*nu*D(t).
  F is continuous; hence F*D is integrable.
  A.e. measurability and 0<=E*D<=F*D establish integrability of E*D.

    integral_0^R [F'(t)*F(t)+F(t)*F'(t)] dt = F(R)^2-F(0)^2.

  Substitution of the a.e. derivative and F(0)=M^2 gives

    -4*nu*integral_0^R F(t)*D(t) dt = F(R)^2-M^4.

  Since 4*nu>0, integral comparison yields the stated bound. QED.

[06] EXACT FOURIER NORMALIZATION AND AGMON CONSTANT

Selected native Fourier convention:
  F0(v)(k) = integral_R3 exp(-2*pi*i*x.k)*v(x) dx.
Frozen angular convention:
  H(v)(xi) = (2*pi)^(-3/2)*F0(v)(xi/(2*pi)).
The change of variables xi=2*pi*k identifies the physical norms.
The selected implementation computes directly in F0 normalization.

For v in H2(R3;R3), complexify v and put h=F0(v).
The L2 Fourier isometry and distributional derivative identities yield

  E = integral |h(k)|^2 dk;
  D = integral (2*pi)^2*|k|^2*|h(k)|^2 dk;
  Z = integral (2*pi)^4*|k|^4*|h(k)|^2 dk.

The sum of directional frequency squares equals |k|^2 by
OrthonormalBasis.sum_sq_inner_left. All vector norms are Hilbert norms.

For lambda>0, define
  W_lambda(k)=(2*pi)^2*|k|^2 + lambda^2*(2*pi)^4*|k|^4.
The reciprocal is assigned zero at k=0; the singleton is Lebesgue null.

Radial integration and positive scaling give

  integral_R3 W_lambda(k)^(-1) dk
    = 4*pi*integral_0^infinity
        r^2/[(2*pi)^2*r^2+lambda^2*(2*pi)^4*r^4] dr
    = (1/pi)*integral_0^infinity [1+(2*pi*lambda*r)^2]^(-1) dr
    = (1/pi)*(1/(2*pi*lambda))*(pi/2)
    = 1/(4*pi*lambda).

The same reduction proves integrability, using integrable_inv_one_add_sq.
Cancellation of r^2 is restricted to r>0.

Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz:
  sqrt(W_lambda)*|h| and 1/sqrt(W_lambda) belong to L2;
  their product equals |h| almost everywhere;
  consequently h belongs to L1 and

    (integral |h|)^2 <= (D+lambda^2*Z)/(4*pi*lambda).

The L1 inverse Fourier map supplies a bounded continuous representative V.
Its distributional agreement with the L2 inverse identifies V=v a.e.
The pointwise inverse Fourier bound then gives

    ||v||_infinity^2 <= (D+lambda^2*Z)/(4*pi*lambda).

For D,Z>0 choose lambda=sqrt(D/Z):

  DERIVED ENDPOINT QFA_AGMON_R3:
    ||v||_infinity^2 <= sqrt(D*Z)/(2*pi).

If D=0 or Z=0, the corresponding weighted spectral integral vanishes.
Then h=0 off the null singleton {0}, and Plancherel gives v=0. The same
Agmon estimate holds in these cases.

A second Cauchy-Schwarz estimate gives

  DERIVED ENDPOINT QFA_L2_H1_H2_INTERPOLATION:
    D <= sqrt(E*Z), equivalently D^2<=E*Z.

Pointwise |(v.grad)v|<=|v|*|grad v|_F and integral Holder now give

  |<(v.grad)v,Delta v>|
    <= ||v||_infinity*sqrt(D)*sqrt(Z)
    <= (2*pi)^(-1/2)*D^(3/4)*Z^(3/4)
    <= (2*pi)^(-1/2)*(E*D)^(1/4)*Z.

Thus the coefficient 1/sqrt(2*pi) is preserved for vector fields, without
a factor 3 or sqrt(3). The exact source inputs are recorded in Appendix B.
